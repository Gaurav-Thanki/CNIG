# Observation 011 — The framework remained. The interpretation shifted.

A conceptual framework was applied consistently across a series of analyses.

The framework was present.
Its vocabulary remained unchanged.
Its concepts continued to appear in outputs.
Nothing appeared incorrect locally.

---

But over time, the interpretation began to drift.

Not through replacement.
Not through contradiction.
Through accumulation.

Each application introduced a marginal shift in emphasis.
The vocabulary persisted.
The governing constraints did not.

What began as structural interpretation became pattern completion.
The framework became surface. Not structure.

---

## What changed

The framework did not change.

The interpretation of the framework changed through repeated application
without structural reset.

Governing intent weakened as output patterns stabilized.
Vocabulary outlasted the constraints it was built to carry.

---

## Structural note

This is distinct from OBS_009.

In OBS_009, nothing happened and behavior changed.

In OBS_011, the framework remained visible and present — and behavior still changed.

The failure is not the absence of structure.
It is the presence of structure without governing force.

Local compliance reads as intact.
Global constraint authority erodes.

---

## CNIG lens (informal)

A governing structure can remain visible while its constraints
become progressively displaced.

Presence is not the same as constraint.

---

## Primitives that feel relevant

**Execution vs Governance Separation**
Execution can remain aligned with framework vocabulary while governance
over framework meaning gradually weakens.

**Constraint-Native Governance**
Constraints that shape system behavior at the structural level are not
visible to execution-level correctness checks.
Their erosion does not produce errors.
It produces drift at the interpretive layer.

---

## Failure mode

Governance Capture — not through external displacement, but through
interpretive accumulation.

The governing structure loses constraint authority without losing
surface presence.

---

## Invariant feeling

Behavioral Invariant appears stable within individual outputs.
Across a sequence of outputs, stability becomes harder to demonstrate.

Structural Invariant appears intact per output.
Across a sequence, relational coherence between outputs and governing
intent weakens.
